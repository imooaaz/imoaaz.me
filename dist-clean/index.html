whoami

I'm a cybersecurity engineer and penetration tester focused on application security, secure SDLC, and DevSecOps. I came to security from software development, and that background still shapes how I work: I read code, not just responses — an exploit makes sense when you understand what the developer intended.

My experience spans vulnerability assessment, bug bounty hunting, and penetration testing as a service — inside product teams at a technology company, and as a consultant working directly with clients.

What I keep sharpening: offensive security, web and mobile application security, secure development practices, and security research — including the security of AI-powered features.
